What We Do

One crew for the whole property.

From the weekly cut to a full yard rebuild to a cleared lot in January, Foreterra handles it — so you're not chasing four different contractors to keep one property looking right.

Mowing

Weekly and bi-weekly cuts on a fixed schedule, with trimming and blow-off included every visit.

Lawn Edging

Crisp, defined lines along walks, drives and beds — the detail that makes a property read as cared-for.

Softscaping

Trees, shrubs, perennials and bed design — planted for the Peace Region climate, not a catalogue.

Hardscaping

Paving stone, retaining walls, decorative rock, concrete curbing and borders built to sit level for good.

Fertilizing

Granular and liquid programs timed to the local season, so the colour lasts past the first hot week.

Herbicide

Targeted weed control for turf and gravel — licensed application, measured rates, no guesswork.

Aeration

Core aeration to break compaction and get water, air and feed down where the roots actually live.

De-thatching

Power raking to pull the dead layer out of the canopy and let a tired lawn breathe again.

Seeding

Overseeding and full sod installs — thin lawns thickened, bare dirt turned into finished turf.

Mulching

Fresh mulch and rock over proper fabric — beds that hold moisture and stay clean through the season.

Snow Removal

Commercial lots, walkways and residential driveways cleared on contract or on call, with sanding and ice control.

Irrigation

Sprinkler installs, zone design, repairs and seasonal blowouts — laid out from a real head-placement plan.

Commercial Contracts

Multi-site condo, retail and institutional maintenance with scheduled service reports and one point of contact.
